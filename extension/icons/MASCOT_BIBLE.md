# Omni mascot — character bible

Source of truth: `extension/icons/logo.png` (mascot holding holographic panel).

## Anatomy (locked)

- **2 arms**, each with **exactly 3 fingers**
- **2 little paws/legs** ending in **golden claws** (same as the icon)
- **2** large cyan almond eyes (glow, no black pupils)
- **1** gold ring / O on the forehead
- **2** black antennae with gold tips
- Round glossy indigo–purple beetle shell
- Large **amber translucent** glowing side panel on the shell
- Default prop: translucent hologram tablet held with both hands

## Rules

- Every wallpaper / scene / mini-mascot must copy this structure — no redesigns.
- Do not invent 4–6 walking legs or five-fingered hands.
- Always use `logo.png` (+ origin wallpaper) as image references when generating.
